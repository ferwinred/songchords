===== APP NAME =====
SongChords: Chords & Lyrics

===== SHORT DESCRIPTION =====
Worship songbook with chords, transposition, a tuner and setlists.

===== FULL DESCRIPTION =====
SongChords is the digital songbook for musicians and praise & worship teams. Keep your songs with lyrics and chords at hand, change them to the key you need, and prepare every service or rehearsal from your phone.

🎵 YOUR SONGBOOK IN ONE PLACE
• Search songs by title or artist.
• Filter by favorites, key, artist or tag (Praise, Worship and more).
• See the tempo (BPM) and time signature of every song.

🎼 INSTANT TRANSPOSITION
• Move up or down a half step or a whole step with one tap.
• Every chord in the song updates automatically.
• Go back to the original key at any time.
• Standard (C D E) or solfège (Do Re Mi) notation.

🎸 CHORD LIBRARY FOR GUITAR AND PIANO
• All 12 keys and 10 chord types: major, minor, 7, m7, maj7, sus2, sus4, add9, dim and aug.
• Clear diagrams showing the fingering for every chord.

🎯 BUILT-IN TUNER
• Tune string by string with your phone's microphone.
• 6-string guitar and 4- and 5-string bass.
• Cents meter and a reference tone for tuning by ear.

📋 SETLISTS FOR YOUR SERVICE
• Create lists for every service, meeting or rehearsal.
• Save the key your team will use for each song in the list.

✍️ WRITE YOUR OWN SONGS
• Put chords in brackets, e.g. [G]When I woke up [C]there.
• Quick insertion helper for chords, sections and comments.
• Live preview with the chords shown above the lyrics.

📤 EXPORT AND SHARE
• Export your songs as PDF, plain text or JSON.
• Import songs from JSON files.
• Sync your songs to the cloud with your Google account.

🤖 AI MUSIC ASSISTANT (Premium AI)
• Ask about scales, chords, progressions and music theory for guitar and piano.

🌙 AND MORE
• Dark mode.
• Available in English and Spanish.

SongChords is free with ads. Optional subscriptions (Basic, Pro and Premium AI) raise the limits on setlists and your own songs, remove ads and unlock the AI assistant.

Download SongChords and take your worship repertoire wherever you go!
